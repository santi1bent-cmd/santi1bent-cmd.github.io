About

A strange loop is a system that moves through its own hierarchy and returns to where it began, transformed. That idea captures my fascination with self-reference, recursion, and emergence, and with how intelligence reflects on itself to become something new. That is the spirit behind A Curious Loop.

My name is Santi Bent. I am an AI safety researcher with a background in molecular biology and clinical medicine, increasingly focused on where AI meets biosecurity.

I hold a bachelor's in biology from the University of Nevada, Reno, and a master's in data science and artificial intelligence from the European Higher Education Institute.

Alongside my research, this site gathers my collection of quotes and essays, where I explore ideas that extend beyond formal technical work.
