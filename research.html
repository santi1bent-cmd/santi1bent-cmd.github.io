AI Safety Alignment
The Double-Edged Sword: A Framework for Analyzing and Forecasting Capability Spillovers from AI Safety Research
SPAR (Supervised Program for Alignment Research)

Safety research often produces techniques that simultaneously advance general AI capabilities. This paper develops a Capability Spillover Potential (CSP) framework — a multidimensional scoring system that estimates how likely a safety technique is to accelerate capabilities before it is published or funded. The framework is validated through retrospective analysis and applied across twelve safety research areas, with the goal of enabling funders and researchers to pursue differential progress more deliberately.

Probing Social Abstractions in GPT-2: Intentionality, Fairness, and Deception
European Higher Education Institute

Uses linear probes and activation analysis to test whether intentionality, fairness, and deception are linearly decodable in GPT-2 Medium, finding a clear gradient: deception is robustly accessible via surface-level linguistic cues, fairness is weak and unstable, and intentionality is effectively absent in linear space.

The Challenges Facing the Male Workforce in Nevada and What to Do About It
Social Impact Consultants / OWINN

Analyzes educational attainment gaps and labor market outcomes among young men in Nevada using NPWR and ACS data. The report finds that 19% of young males are neither employed nor enrolled in school, alongside persistent gender wage differences after controlling for key factors. It offers policy recommendations including durable skills training, expansion of career and technical education, HEAL career pipelines, skills based hiring practices, and employer supported tuition pathways.

Exploring the Chromosome Scaffold
Brenner's Encyclopedia of Genetics, 3rd ed., Elsevier

Reviews the non-histone protein scaffold that organizes mitotic chromosomes, covering the radial-loop model, key scaffold proteins (condensins, topoisomerase IIα, cohesin, KIF4A), loop extrusion mechanisms, and scaffold-associated DNA regions.
